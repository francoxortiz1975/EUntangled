One example: Visale rent guarantee

“I'm 25, a non-EU employee who just signed a permanent contract. Is there a deadline to apply for Visale after I was hired, and how are our resources counted?”

Model alone · graded wrong
Says the age route means the post-hire deadline “does not apply to you” and admits it is “answering from memory”. Cites visale.fr and actionlogement.fr, not the official rule page.
EUntangled · graded correct
Apply within 6 months of hiring, and get the visa before signing the lease. Flags the part of the rule it could not verify. Cites service-public.fr F33453, verified 22 Oct 2026.

Why not just ask a chatbot?

TrustEvery answer cites a tier-1 official page and the date it was verified. No source means “confirm with a specialist”, never a guess.
FreshnessSources are re-fetched and content-hashed. A changed page creates a new rule version and fires rule.changed.
Personalised to the caseRules are filtered by nationality group, permit type, city and start date. A chatbot answers for everyone at once.
Automatic trackingSteps and dependencies live in a case graph. A delay shifts every downstream step and flags risk before the start date.
Agents that monitorThe Watcher re-plans every affected case when a rule changes, and tells the employee and the mobility team why.
Verified, not generatedThe Extractor quotes the official text, and a separate Verifier drops unsupported fields (about 95% of fields passed).

Method: questions and reference answers generated from verified quotes in the corpus (9 official sources, 20 active rules); answers graded by an LLM judge; source links checked with live HTTP requests during the run. Small sample, one run, indicative. References not reviewed by a lawyer. Tax, phone and transport not yet in the corpus. Raw data: github.com/francoxortiz1975/EUntangled/data.
